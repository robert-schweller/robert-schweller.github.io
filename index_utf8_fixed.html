I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Northwestern University in June 2007 and my B.A. in Mathematics from Carleton College in June 2001.

 

Research Interests:
My general research area is the design and analysis of algorithms. More specific interests include the algorithmic self-assembly of DNA, chemical reaction networks, and robot motion planning.
