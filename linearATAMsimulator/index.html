REFERENCE

Model & methods

This workspace follows the seeded probabilistic linear model in Linear Assemblies: Expected and High Probability (project paper, November 2026).

One-dimensional seeded growth

A single seed has an inert west side. Tiles attach irreversibly at the exposed east end. North and south sides are inert, and tiles cannot rotate. Total length L includes the seed. Assemblies never attach to one another.

Multiplicity and probability

Each distinct nonseed tile has a positive integer multiplicity μ. Among compatible tiles, attachment probability is μ divided by the sum of compatible multiplicities. Failed draws are skipped: they change waiting time, not assembly length. Charged complexity is m = 1 + Σ μ, including the seed.

One-pad

West and east labels bind when equal and noninert.

Two-pad

Either corresponding pad may match. A tile matching both positions is still counted once. Write two labels as a | b.

Flexible glues

Only explicitly listed label pairs bind. Pairs are symmetric; equality alone does not imply compatibility. Inert labels never bind.

Construction and exact statistics

Generate uses the explicit positive-ternary one-pad construction in Section 3.1, with separate endpoint labels for every digit of N − 1. Its expectation equals N exactly. This is a constructive upper bound; the app does not certify a minimum tile set.

For this construction, expectation and variance use integer recurrences, including for enormous N. Custom systems use exact rational absorbing Markov chain equations for up to 64 reachable nonterminal interfaces. Reachability determines whether termination is almost sure.

Trials and limits

The random seed makes trials reproducible. A trial stops when no compatible tile exists, or is marked capped at the attachment limit. Capped lengths are lower bounds on terminal length, never reported as completed outcomes. Each batch has a 10-million-attachment work budget; unstarted trials are counted separately.

Graphical traces retain at most 5,000 attachments. Generated targets above 100,000 default to numerical analysis. Predicted distributions use floating-point Markov propagation, limited to 20,000 lengths and 128 reachable states; any unresolved tail is reported. Standard deviations and scientific-notation summaries are approximate; exact moments are available in Analysis.
